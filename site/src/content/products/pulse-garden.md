Pulse Garden is a turn-based strategy puzzle built around a single idea: **one tap plants an
organism, and a web of reactions changes the entire garden.**

Every move resolves predictably, so you can learn exactly why a chain worked — and then plan a
bigger one. Stones block planting and pulses. Fertile soil doubles points. Instability rises
with every organism you plant; playing hot multiplies your score, until the garden overgrows.

Each garden has a *par*: the score reached by a built-in player on the same garden. One star
opens the next level; three stars means you matched it.

Pulse Garden is built in Godot and designed for a phone held in one hand. It is still in
development, and nothing here is final.
